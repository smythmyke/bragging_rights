# Privacy Policy - Additions for Freemium Model

**Document Purpose:** Add these sections to your existing Privacy Policy

## Section: Prize and Competition Data

### 1. Winner Information Collection

We collect additional information from competition winners:

**1.1 Verification Data**
- Government-issued ID (for age verification)
- Full legal name
- Mailing address
- Date of birth

**Purpose:** Verify eligibility and deliver prizes

**Retention:** 7 years (for tax and legal compliance)

**1.2 Tax Reporting Data**
For prizes exceeding $600 cumulative value in a calendar year:
- Social Security Number or Tax Identification Number
- Completed W-9 form
- Employment status
- Citizenship/residency status

**Purpose:** Comply with IRS reporting requirements (Form 1099-MISC)

**Retention:** 7 years (per IRS requirements)

**Sharing:** Only with IRS and state tax authorities as required by law

**1.3 Prize Acceptance Records**
- Prize claim timestamp
- Prize type and value
- Account status at time of award
- Acceptance/declination response

**Purpose:** Fraud prevention, audit compliance, dispute resolution

**Retention:** 3 years minimum

## Section: Leaderboard and Public Display

### 2. Public Information Display

**2.1 What We Display Publicly**
When you participate in competitions, the following may be displayed:
- Username (chosen by you)
- Avatar/profile picture (if uploaded)
- City and State (not full address)
- Competition rank and score
- Achievement badges and trophies
- Win/loss record
- "Premium" badge (if subscribed)

**2.2 What We Do NOT Display**
- Full legal name (unless you use it as username)
- Email address
- Payment information
- Detailed address
- Phone number
- Date of birth
- Specific prize amounts won

**2.3 Opting Out of Public Display**
You can control visibility:
- Set username to pseudonym (Settings > Profile)
- Hide location (Settings > Privacy > Hide Location)
- Set profile to private (excludes you from public leaderboards but not in-app competition rankings)

**Note:** Competition participation requires basic leaderboard display (at minimum, username and rank). Complete anonymity not possible while actively competing.

## Section: Payment and Subscription Data

### 3. Financial Information

**3.1 In-App Purchases (BR Currency)**
We collect:
- Purchase timestamp
- Amount purchased
- Currency type (BR)
- Payment method type (Apple Pay, Google Pay, credit card - last 4 digits only)
- Transaction ID

**Processing:** Apple App Store and Google Play Store process all payments. We never receive or store your full credit card numbers.

**Purpose:** Track purchases, prevent fraud, fulfill transactions, customer support

**Retention:** 7 years (for accounting and tax compliance)

**3.2 Premium Subscriptions**
We collect:
- Subscription start date
- Subscription tier (monthly or annual)
- Renewal dates
- Cancellation date (if applicable)
- Subscription status (active, cancelled, expired, trial)
- Auto-renewal preference

**Purpose:** Manage subscriptions, process renewals, provide customer support

**Retention:** Duration of subscription + 3 years

**3.3 Refunds and Chargebacks**
We log:
- Refund requests and outcomes
- Chargeback disputes
- Customer support tickets related to billing

**Purpose:** Fraud prevention, dispute resolution, customer support

**Retention:** 7 years

## Section: Third-Party Data Sources

### 4. Odds and Sports Data Providers

**4.1 The Odds API (Premium Users Only)**
When you use premium features, we fetch odds data from The Odds API:

**Data Shared with Provider:**
- Sport type requested
- Event/game ID
- Timestamp of request
- Your approximate location (country/state - to provide region-specific odds)

**Data NOT Shared:**
- Your personal identity
- Your username or email
- Your picks or predictions
- Your account details

**Provider's Privacy Policy:** https://the-odds-api.com/privacy

**Purpose:** Provide real-time betting odds for premium scoring

**4.2 ESPN and TheSportsDB (All Users)**
We fetch free sports data (scores, schedules, team records):

**Data Shared:**
- API requests are anonymous
- No personal information transmitted
- Only sport and game identifiers

**Purpose:** Provide game schedules, scores, and team statistics

## Section: Data Retention

### 7. How Long We Keep Your Data

**7.1 Active Accounts**
While your account is active, we retain:
- All account information
- Competition history (indefinitely)
- Prize records (7 years minimum)
- Payment history (7 years)
- Support tickets (3 years)

**7.2 Deleted Accounts**
When you delete your account:
- Personal information deleted within 30 days
- Competition data anonymized (username replaced with "Deleted User")
- Prize/tax records retained for 7 years (legal requirement)
- Payment records retained for 7 years (accounting requirement)

**7.3 Inactive Accounts**
If inactive for 365+ days:
- We may email you to confirm account status
- Account may be flagged for deletion after 730 days (2 years)
- 30-day notice before permanent deletion

## Section: Data Security

### 8. How We Protect Your Information

**8.1 Security Measures**
- Encryption in transit (TLS/SSL)
- Encryption at rest (AES-256)
- Firebase Security Rules (database access control)
- Regular security audits
- Employee access limited to need-to-know basis
- Two-factor authentication for admin accounts

**8.2 Payment Security**
- We never store full credit card numbers
- Apple and Google handle all payment processing
- PCI DSS compliance through app store providers

**8.3 Third-Party Security**
Our providers maintain:
- SOC 2 Type II certification (Firebase, AWS)
- ISO 27001 certification
- Regular penetration testing
- 24/7 security monitoring

**8.4 Data Breach Protocol**
If a breach occurs, we will:
- Notify affected users within 72 hours
- Notify relevant authorities as required by law
- Provide details of compromised data
- Offer identity protection services (if applicable)
- Publish public incident report

## Section: Your Privacy Rights

### 10. Your Data Rights

**10.1 Right to Access**
Request a copy of all data we have about you:
- Settings > Privacy > Download My Data
- Delivered within 30 days in machine-readable format (JSON)

**10.2 Right to Correction**
Update inaccurate information:
- Settings > Profile (for basic info)
- Contact support@braggingrights.app for other corrections

**10.3 Right to Deletion**
Request account deletion:
- Settings > Account > Delete Account
- Most data deleted within 30 days
- Some records retained for legal compliance (7 years)

**10.4 Right to Object**
Object to data processing:
- Opt out of marketing emails
- Disable analytics tracking
- Request manual review of automated decisions

**10.5 Right to Portability**
Export your data in portable format:
- Competition history (CSV)
- Pick records (JSON)
- Account information (JSON)

**10.6 State-Specific Rights**

**California Residents (CCPA):**
- Right to know what data is collected
- Right to delete data
- Right to opt out of data sales (we don't sell data)
- Right to non-discrimination

**European Residents (GDPR):**
- Right to access
- Right to rectification
- Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
- Right to data portability
- Right to object
- Right to restrict processing

**To exercise these rights:** privacy@braggingrights.app
