🎮 Watch Live - BR Charge UI Options

Three different approaches to charging BR for third-party streaming links

Option 1: Unlock All Links
RECOMMENDED

Pros

  • Simple, one-time decision
  • User-friendly experience
  • No repeated payments
  • Clear value proposition
  • Predictable revenue per session

Cons

  • Lower total revenue per user
  • Users might not visit all links
  • No differentiation between links

Option 2: Pay Per Click
REVENUE MAX

Pros

  • Higher revenue potential
  • Users only pay for what they use
  • Fair pricing model
  • Encourages link quality

Cons

  • Friction on every click
  • May frustrate users
  • Requires wallet check each time
  • Could discourage exploration
  • More complex UX

Option 3: Time-Based Access Tiers
FLEXIBLE

Pros

  • Flexible pricing options
  • Recurring revenue potential
  • Premium tier for power users
  • Users choose commitment level
  • Gamification opportunity

Cons

  • Complex to manage
  • Requires timer/expiry system
  • May confuse casual users
  • Need clear value differentiation

💡 Recommendation

Option 1 (Unlock All) is recommended for the best user experience. It's simple, clear, and removes friction while still monetizing access. The one-time payment model respects users' time and creates a positive association with BR.

Option 3 (Time-Based) could be a great alternative if you want to encourage regular usage and create recurring revenue streams, especially for power users who watch multiple games per week.

Option 2 (Pay Per Click) maximizes revenue but may frustrate users and discourage exploration. Best avoided unless you have very high-quality, differentiated links.
